Version 0.9.9 · Build 2
Changes since 0.9.9 Build 1

Witty schedule in Windows status
- Show schedule information only for attached Witty Pi 3, 4 or 5 hardware with matching installed manufacturer software.
- Display next shutdown and startup on separate lines as HH:MM      DD.MM.YYYY, followed by minutes until shutdown.
- Read schedule data only on successful connection/reconnection. Thereafter compute the countdown locally from the shutdown time and current Windows PC clock, without additional SSH or Witty queries.
- Reuse homepage readings up to 30 seconds old. Without a recent cache, obtain one shared backend status reading on connection.
- Deactivate schedule uses the existing homepage backend. Clear displayed events/countdown only after confirmed success; saved schedule files are retained.

Validation
- Tests cover Witty 3/4/5 hardware/install gating, date format, PC countdown, inactive schedules, success/failure of deactivation and cache storage/invalidation.
- Native Windows compilation validates the build. Verify the new row and event times against the homepage on the device.
